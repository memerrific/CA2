About CA2

Built around doing the job properly.

Founded in 2019, CA2 has grown through hands-on work for builders, homeowners and renovation projects. The direction is specialist mechanical and electrical building services—without losing the practical plumbing knowledge that makes those systems work in the real world.

That means looking beyond an individual boiler, pump or control. Heat loss, pipe sizing, emitters, hydraulics, wiring, commissioning and future maintenance all influence the result.
