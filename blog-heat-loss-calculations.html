Heating design

Why room-by-room heat-loss calculations matter.

A heating system should be based on what each room loses on a cold design day—not the output of the appliance that happened to be there before.

What the calculation considers

Room dimensions, exposed walls, windows, doors, floors, roofs, ventilation and the thermal performance of the construction all affect the rate of heat loss. The design temperatures inside and outside must also be stated.

Why whole-house estimates are not enough

A total building figure can help size the heat source, but it does not show how much heat each room needs. Room figures are needed to select radiators, assess usable underfloor-heating area and balance distribution correctly.

How it improves the specification

  • Reduces the risk of oversized or undersized equipment
  • Supports lower, more efficient flow temperatures
  • Highlights rooms that need emitter or fabric improvements
  • Provides a basis for pipe sizing and system flow
  • Makes design decisions easier to explain and document
