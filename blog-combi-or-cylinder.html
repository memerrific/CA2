Hot water · System choice

Combi boiler or unvented cylinder?

The right answer depends on the incoming main, the number of bathrooms and how the household uses hot water.

When a combi can work well

A combi heats hot water as it is used and does not need a storage cylinder. It can suit smaller homes with moderate demand, but it cannot create more incoming flow or pressure than the water main can provide.

Why stored hot water helps

An unvented cylinder stores heated water and can serve several outlets more effectively when the incoming main and distribution pipework are suitable. Cylinder volume, recovery rate and heat-source output must be matched to the property.

Check simultaneous demand

Two showers, a bath fill and kitchen use can overlap in a busy home. A site survey should measure static and dynamic pressure, flow rate and existing pipe sizes before equipment is selected.

Allow for the complete installation

Space, discharge arrangements, controls, maintenance access and future heat-pump plans all influence the decision. The best system is the one designed around actual demand rather than a product label.
