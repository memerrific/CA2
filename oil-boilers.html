How we approach it

An annual service keeps an oil boiler burning cleanly and catches small faults before they become breakdowns. When a boiler is near the end of its life, we'll set out the real options, including whether your home is suited to a heat pump and what that would involve.

CA2 is OFTEC qualified for oil-fired equipment.

Common questions

Before you book.

How often should an oil boiler be serviced?

Once a year. Many manufacturers require it to keep the warranty valid, and it keeps combustion clean and efficient.

What's included in a service?

Cleaning the burner and heat exchanger, replacing the nozzle and filters, checking controls and safety devices, and testing combustion. See our guide for the full list.

Should I replace my oil boiler with a heat pump?

It depends on the house and your plans. We can assess both options so you can compare them.
