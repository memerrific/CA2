Cookie policy

This page explains the current use of cookies and similar browser technologies on the CA2 website.

Current position

CA2 does not currently run Google Analytics or advertising trackers on this temporary website. Optional analytics will not be activated unless a valid measurement ID and a consent mechanism are configured.

Essential preference storage

If optional analytics is enabled in future, the website may store a necessary preference in your browser to remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics. This preference is used only to honour your choice.

Third-party content

The Contact page offers content supplied by Tradify and Google Maps. Those services are not loaded until you select the relevant load button. Once loaded, the third party may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. You can instead use the direct contact details without loading either service.

Future analytics

Before Google Analytics or another non-essential analytics service is activated, visitors will be offered equally clear accept and reject choices. Non-essential analytics will remain off unless accepted.

Managing browser data

You can remove stored website data through your browser settings. Blocking all browser storage may affect choices or features that rely on strictly necessary preferences.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to enquiries@ca2plumbing.com.
