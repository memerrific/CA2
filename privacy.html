Privacy policy

This policy explains how CA2 Heating & Plumbing Ltd collects and uses personal information received through this website and direct enquiries.

Who is responsible

CA2 Heating & Plumbing Ltd, company number 12029657, is the data controller. Registered office: 124 City Road, London City Road, London, England, EC1V 2NX. Privacy enquiries may be sent to enquiries@ca2plumbing.com.

Information we may receive

This may include your name, contact details, property or site location, photographs, plans, booking preferences, project details, correspondence, quotation and payment records, and information needed to carry out or support the requested work.

Why and how information is used

Information may be processed to respond to an enquiry, take steps requested before entering a contract, provide contracted services, manage accounts and records, comply with legal duties, and pursue legitimate business interests such as customer service, security and resolving disputes. Where consent is the appropriate basis, it may be withdrawn at any time.

Who information may be shared with

Where reasonably necessary, information may be shared with employees, subcontractors, professional advisers, software and hosting providers, merchants, manufacturers, payment providers or public authorities. Information is not sold. Third-party providers process information under their own terms or under arrangements with CA2, as applicable.

Website services and international processing

The website uses or links to services including GitHub Pages, Tradify, Google Maps, WhatsApp and Instagram. Loading or following third-party services may send technical information to those providers and may involve processing outside the UK. Embedded Tradify and Google content is not loaded until a visitor chooses to load it. Google Analytics is currently inactive.

Retention

Enquiry information that does not lead to work is kept only while reasonably needed to respond and follow up. Contract, quotation, invoice, certification, warranty and accounting records may be retained for the periods required by law and for establishing or defending legal claims. Records are then deleted or securely disposed of when no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your information, object to processing, receive certain information in a portable format, and withdraw consent. To exercise a right, contact CA2 using the address above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Cookies and similar technologies

Read the separate cookie policy for the current website position and the controls that will apply before optional analytics is enabled.
