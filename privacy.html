Privacy policy

This working policy explains the information CA2 Heating & Plumbing Ltd receives through this website and direct enquiries.

Who is responsible

CA2 Heating & Plumbing Ltd, company number 12029657, is responsible for personal information used to respond to enquiries and provide services. Contact: enquiries@ca2plumbing.com.

Information we may receive

This may include your name, contact details, property or site location, photographs, plans, booking preferences, project details and correspondence.

How information is used

Information is used to answer enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange work, provide contracted services, keep appropriate business records and meet legal or regulatory duties.

Who information may be shared with

Where necessary for the requested work, information may be shared with relevant employees, subcontractors, professional advisers, software providers, merchants, manufacturers or authorities. Information is not sold.

Website services

The website links to services including WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps. Those providers operate under their own privacy terms. Google Analytics is not active until a valid measurement ID and an appropriate consent arrangement are configured.

Retention and your rights

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, contractual records and legal obligations. You may contact CA2 to ask about access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
