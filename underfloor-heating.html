Underfloor heating

Underfloor heating, designed for your floor

Underfloor heating gives even, comfortable warmth and frees up wall space. It works best when it is designed from the room's heat loss and the floor construction, not laid to a standard pattern.

How we approach it

The floor construction decides the method. Screed floors hold heat and respond slowly; timber floors need spreader plates and respond faster. We design loop lengths and spacing for each room, and set the flow temperature to suit the floor finish and the heat source.

Common questions

Before you book.

Can underfloor heating go upstairs?

Yes. Timber floors use spreader plates or routed boards, designed for the joist layout and floor finish.

Will it work with my boiler?

Yes, usually with a mixing unit to lower the water temperature. It also pairs very well with a heat pump.

What floor finishes work?

Tile and stone work best. Engineered wood, vinyl and carpet work too, within the manufacturer's temperature and thermal-resistance limits.
