Water systems

Improving water pressure and flow at home.

The right solution starts with measurements. Pressure, flow, storage and peak demand describe different parts of the problem.

Measure before selecting equipment

Static pressure shows the supply when outlets are closed. Dynamic pressure shows what remains while water is moving. Flow tests help establish how much water can actually be delivered at the point of measurement.

Accumulator tanks

An accumulator can store a volume of mains water under pressure and release it during short periods of higher demand. The useful volume and recharge conditions need to suit the property rather than simply fitting the largest vessel that will fit.

Break tanks and booster sets

Where demand or supply conditions require stored water and pumped distribution, a correctly sized break tank and packaged booster set may be more appropriate. Space, noise, electrical supply, overflow and maintenance access all need consideration.

Look at the whole system

Restricted internal pipework, filters, valves, water-treatment equipment and simultaneous outlet use can all affect performance. Equipment should be specified around the measured system and intended demand.
