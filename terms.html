01

General works

The core terms intended to accompany every accepted CA2 quotation.

Website and quotations

  • Website descriptions are general information and do not by themselves form a quotation, booking confirmation or contract.
  • The written quotation, accepted scope, drawings, specifications and stated terms record the work agreed for an individual project.
  • A booking request or preferred date is not confirmed until CA2 accepts it directly.

Scope, competence and registrations

  • Work is undertaken within CA2’s competence and current registrations. Where regulated work requires a separate registration or specialist, it will be carried out or certified by an appropriately qualified and registered person.
  • Logos, qualifications and memberships apply only while current and only to the activities within their scope.
  • Work by other trades, designers or specialists remains their responsibility unless the accepted quotation expressly places that responsibility with CA2.

Customer contracts

  • Customers will receive the price, payment arrangements, scope, important exclusions and other required pre-contract information before becoming bound.
  • Where a consumer cancellation right applies, the relevant notice and cancellation information will be supplied in a form the customer can retain.
  • If a consumer asks for services to begin during a cancellation period, CA2 will obtain the required express request and explain any reasonable payment that may become due.

Project administration

  • Quotation validity, acceptance, deposits, stage payments, VAT and overdue amounts.
  • Customer access, parking, utilities, working hours and protection of possessions.
  • Variations, discoveries hidden behind existing finishes and work by other trades.
  • Completion, defects, warranties, ownership of materials and dispute handling.

03

Boiler replacements & system conversions

For like-for-like replacements and conversions between stored-water, conventional and combination-boiler arrangements.

Important system outcomes to agree before work

  • Hot-water delivery time: a combination boiler does not provide instant hot water at every outlet. Delivery time depends on the pipe route, pipe volume and distance between the boiler and tap.
  • Flow and pressure: performance is limited by the incoming mains supply and property pipework. A boiler conversion does not by itself increase the available incoming flow rate or pressure.
  • Existing shower pumps: equipment designed for a stored-water arrangement may be removed or become unsuitable following conversion.
  • Boosting: if the measured supply and proposed arrangement indicate that additional boosting or storage is desirable, this will be specified and priced separately unless expressly included.
  • Existing system condition: new equipment can reveal restrictions, leaks or weaknesses in older radiators, valves and pipework. Any remedial work outside the accepted scope is treated as a variation.

Still to tailor

Flue routes, condensate, controls, cleaning, commissioning, warranties, making good and customer-selected boiler outputs.

Heat pumps, electric heating & renewables

For low-temperature heating, heat pumps, thermal storage and associated electrical or hydraulic work.

Drafting scope

  • Heat-loss information, design conditions and customer-supplied energy data.

  • Running-cost illustrations are estimates, not guaranteed bills.

Water treatment, filtration & boosting

For softeners, filters, Aquabion units, accumulator tanks, pumps and packaged booster sets.

Drafting scope

  • Equipment selection relies on measured supply conditions and the stated peak demand.
  • Water treatment changes specific characteristics but does not guarantee every taste, staining or water-quality outcome.
  • Filters, salt and other consumables require ongoing customer maintenance and replacement.
  • Noise, usable storage volume, electrical supplies, discharge routes and space requirements.

Using these terms on a quotation

The final quotation should identify the applicable section, its revision date and any job-specific departures. A web link should supplement—not replace—the key price, scope and cancellation information supplied to the customer in a form they can retain.
