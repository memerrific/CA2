Frequently asked questions.

Practical information before arranging heating, plumbing or renovation work with CA2.

What areas does CA2 cover?

CA2 is based in Sussex and works across the county and surrounding areas. For larger mechanical or renovation projects, contact us with the site location so travel and programme requirements can be considered.

Do you provide quotations?

Yes. The level of detail needed depends on the work. Straightforward repairs may begin with a service visit, while installations and renovations normally require photographs, plans or a site survey before a written quotation.

Can CA2 design a complete heating system?

Yes. The scope can include heat-loss calculations, pipe sizing, emitters, underfloor heating, zoning, controls, hot-water storage and heat-source integration.

Do you work with builders and other trades?

Yes. CA2 regularly coordinates service routes, first fix, testing and final connections with builders and electricians on renovation projects.

Can you help with poor water pressure?

Yes. The correct solution depends on measured incoming flow and pressure, demand and available storage space. Options can include accumulator tanks, DAB pumps or packaged booster sets.

Do you install water softeners and filters?

Yes. CA2 installs water softeners, salt systems, carbon and drinking-water filtration, Aquabion scale-protection systems and associated servicing.

Will a combi boiler improve my water pressure?

Not automatically. A combination boiler relies on the incoming mains supply. Available flow, static and dynamic pressure, pipe sizing and outlet demand should be assessed before conversion.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We aim to reply within one working day. We may request photographs, plans, equipment details or arrange a survey before confirming the next step.
