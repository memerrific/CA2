Underfloor heating design

Underfloor heating in screed and suspended timber floors.

Both floor types can work well, but they store and release heat differently and need an installation method suited to their construction.

Screeded floors

Pipe is commonly fixed over insulation before the screed is installed. The screed spreads heat and adds thermal mass, producing steady operation but a slower response to changes.

Suspended timber floors

These systems often use aluminium spreader plates or purpose-made panels to transfer heat into the floor. Insulation position, contact with the floor deck and final floor covering are important to output and response.

One property can need both

Extensions and renovations frequently combine new screed with existing timber construction. Separate loop design, balanced flow and carefully positioned controls help the areas work together.

Design before installation

  • Confirm room heat losses and active floor area
  • Check floor build-up and insulation
  • Account for kitchens, fixed furniture and floor finishes
  • Plan manifold position, loop lengths and zoning
  • Commission and balance every circuit
