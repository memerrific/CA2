Can CA2 design the complete heating system?

Yes. Work can include heat loss, pipe sizing, emitters, UFH, controls and hot-water integration.

Will a combi improve my water pressure?

Not automatically. Incoming flow, dynamic pressure, pipework and demand must be assessed first.
